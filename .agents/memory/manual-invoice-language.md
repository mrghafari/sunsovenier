---
name: Manual invoice language
description: Keep manually generated invoice PDFs and invoice emails aligned with the operator's intended language.
---

For staff-issued shop invoices, preview, print, and download use the current site language. When sending by email, staff explicitly choose one language for both the PDF attachment and the email subject/body; the initial selection follows the site language. Do not silently replace that choice with the customer's stored language.

**Why:** Staff need to see a PDF in the language they selected on the site, while the recipient's preferred language is not necessarily the language staff intend for a particular emailed invoice.

**How to apply:** Pass the language explicitly for every manually generated invoice PDF and require a validated language on manual send. Other automatic customer notifications may still follow the customer's stored preference.

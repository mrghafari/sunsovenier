name: Contract packaging billing
description: User-selected packaging fee basis and historical order policy.
---

Contract packaging is charged per tonne of the goods' **net** weight, with its own VAT percentage. Add the fee before VAT and never charge goods VAT on it.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “به‌ازای هر تن وزن خالص کالا” and requested a separate packaging VAT coefficient.

**How to apply:** Keep packaging separate from goods prices and shipping. Do not substitute gross/shipping weight or invent missing net weights. Freeze the calculation and its weight/rate evidence on each order; later contract/addendum/catalog edits must not reprice prior orders. Legacy contracts without packaging remain unchanged.

---
name: Retiring used contract lines
description: Meaning of deleting a product from a sales contract after quantities were used.
---

Removing a product from a contract must make it unavailable for future contract purchases. If units were ever allocated, preserve the historical line rather than physically deleting it; unused lines may be deleted.

**Why:** Prior orders, holds, and released quantities depend on the allocation record. Physical deletion of a used line could erase the evidence needed to reconcile commitments and refunds.

**How to apply:** Treat the customer's active contract lines as the sellable set; keep retired lines available to internal reconciliation only. Do not let a removed product reappear through catalog eligibility, search, or a checkout claim.

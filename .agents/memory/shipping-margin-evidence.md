---
name: Shipping margin evidence
description: Why booked shipping contribution is not inferred operational profit.
---

Shipping margins are booked, source-linked contributions, not inferred physical
profit. Capturing a loading/cost does not establish that its financial document
was posted; absent cost is unknown, not zero.

**Why:** The operational bridge was deliberately permitted as capture-only while
financial activation remains closed. Existing invoice history does not reliably
identify individual trucks/loadings, and inferred attribution can manufacture
profit or count only the completed portion of a multi-loading order.

**How to apply:** Require explicit immutable source attribution for loading-level
financial comparisons. Keep unlinked history visible rather than allocating it
to a convenient loading. Compare original native cost evidence, not reversal-net
amounts or today's fleet rates, and describe all resulting figures as booked
amounts rather than physical verification.

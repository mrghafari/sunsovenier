## Business rules (fixed decisions)
- First country: Iraq. Functional (base) currency IQD. Multi-currency must be supported by design (transaction currency, rate, base amount on every journal line). Use official central-bank rates stored in `exchange_rates`. Use Gregorian dates for Iraq; store DATE/UTC, display per user locale. Company has `countryCode` and `timezone` (Asia/Baghdad).
- Country-specific logic (tax rules, chart of accounts template, e-invoicing) must sit behind a country-pack interface so other countries can be added later.
- Tax: do NOT hardcode a VAT rate. Iraq may currently have no general VAT. Tax rate comes from `tax_rules` (country, product group, validity dates) and is snapshotted on the order line at order creation. Rate 0 or no rule = no tax lines.
- Pricing: price, tax rate and shipping terms are LOCKED at order creation (snapshot on the order line). Invoice amount = ACTUAL loaded weight (weighbridge) × locked unit price, plus shipping and tax per snapshot. Add a configurable weight tolerance (%) setting: within tolerance auto-invoice; beyond it requires finance approval.
- Revenue recognition: at loading completion (`LoadingCompleted`), per loading (one order may have several loadings/trucks). Warehouse approval only reserves stock; it creates no journal entry.
- Invoices ALWAYS post first to Accounts Receivable, then get settled (by wallet or receipt), even if settled instantly.
- Wallet: customer prepaid balance, single currency per customer. It is a LIABILITY (control account WALLET_LIABILITY), never revenue. On finance approval, HOLD (block) the order total (goods + shipping + tax) in `wallet_holds` for wallet-paying orders; holds have no journal entry. Loading consumes the hold; leftover hold is released when the order is closed/cancelled. Wallet subledger must reconcile with the GL control account (build a daily reconciliation report). Order currency must equal wallet currency.
- Credit sales exist (payment terms in days → due date). Finance approval must check: open receivables + uncleared cheques + new order amount ≤ customer credit limit (add `credit_limit` per customer/currency if missing).
- Shipping: each order has `shippingMode` = CUSTOMER_ARRANGED or SELLER_ARRANGED. For SELLER_ARRANGED, shipping price is locked on the order (per unit or fixed, fixed is split across loadings) and posts to SHIPPING_REVENUE. Actual shipping cost is recorded separately per loading via `ShipmentCostRecorded`: EXTERNAL_CARRIER → Dr SHIPPING_EXPENSE / Cr CARRIER_PAYABLE (party = carrier); INTERNAL_FLEET → Dr SHIPPING_EXPENSE (fleet cost center) / Cr FLEET_COST_ABSORBED at an internal rate (per ton or per km, configurable). Real fleet costs (fuel, driver, depreciation) are posted to the fleet cost center via manual journal entries; build a period report of absorbed vs actual (over/under absorption) and shipping margin per loading/order.
- Ledger integrity: double-entry, balanced on BASE amounts, posted entries immutable, corrections only by reversal entries, period locking via fiscal years, idempotency by (sourceType, sourceId), all money via decimal.js / numeric (never JS number/float), per-currency decimals from `currencies.decimals` (IQD = 0 by default, configurable).
- Account mapping lives in `posting_rules` (key → account), never hardcoded account IDs in code.

## Acceptance criteria
- Duplicate events never create duplicate journal entries; consumer retries safely and records errors.
- Any attempt to UPDATE/DELETE a posted entry or line fails at DB level; unbalanced entries fail at DB level.
- A loading of 24.350 tons produces correct sales, shipping revenue, optional tax, COGS, receivable item with due date, and wallet settlement when funds are held.
- Trial balance always balances; wallet subledger equals WALLET_LIABILITY balance per currency.
- Existing order/finance/warehouse/logistics features keep working unchanged.
- No floats for money anywhere; no hardcoded tax rate or account IDs.

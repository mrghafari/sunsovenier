## CORE WORKFLOW (strict state machine; each step unlocks only after the previous one is confirmed)
1. ORDER_CREATED: Customer/sales creates an order (product, grade, quantity, packaging type: bag/pallet or bulk, delivery destination). Check inventory and credit.
2. CARRIER_ASSIGNED: Customer selects and registers their own carrier company, then enters driver info (name, national ID, license number and expiry, phone) and vehicle info (plate, type, capacity, tare weight, insurance and technical inspection expiry, e-waybill number). The system auto-validates: documents not expired, driver HSE training valid, vehicle compatible with product, driver/vehicle/carrier not blacklisted. Reject with a clear reason if validation fails.
3. ENTRY_PERMIT_ISSUED: System assigns an appointment time slot and issues a ONE-TIME entry permit with a QR code (and a simulated SMS-style notification).
4. MAIN_GATE_IN: Gate officer scans QR / enters plate; system verifies driver, vehicle and permit match. Safety checklist (spark arrestor, tires, extinguisher, phone/lighter handed in). Record time, photo upload, issue a pass card. Status: inside plant, waiting.
5. WAITING_YARD: Truck appears in a queue ordered by priority, arrival time and product readiness. Warehouse manager calls trucks (CALLED status). A truck that has not been called cannot proceed.
6. WAREHOUSE_GATE_IN: Warehouse gate officer verifies the call and the safety permit, records entry.
7. TARE_WEIGH (Weighbridge 1): Operator records empty weight (input or "simulate reading" button). Compare to registered tare weight; if deviation exceeds a configurable threshold (e.g. 3%), raise a warning requiring supervisor approval with a reason.
8. LOADING: System assigns a loading bay/silo/dock. Loading operator confirms start. For bag/pallet: count by barcode scans or counter input and verify batch number. For bulk: record flowmeter/loadcell quantity with auto-stop at the ordered amount. Verify product and batch match the order. At the end, record the seal number.
9. GROSS_WEIGH (Weighbridge 2): Record full weight. Net weight = gross − tare. Compare with ordered quantity and counted/flowmeter quantity within a configurable tolerance. If outside tolerance, send the truck back to loading with a mandatory reason. Generate a weighbridge ticket.
10. DOCUMENTS_ISSUED: Auto-generate printable documents (PDF): waybill, invoice/delivery order, Certificate of Analysis (CoA), MSDS, weighbridge ticket. Deduct inventory via InventoryAdapter.
11. WAREHOUSE_GATE_OUT: Verify documents, seal, plate and weight match.
12. MAIN_GATE_OUT: Final inspection, collect and invalidate the pass card, record exit time. Status CLOSED. The permit can never be reused. Notify the customer.

## BUSINESS RULES
- No loading without a tare weight; no exit without a gross weight and approval.
- Each loading has a unique Loading ID; every step records user, timestamp, and optional photo.
- Exceptions: re-weigh, return to loading, cancel loading, overload, incompatible vehicle. These require higher-level approval and a reason.
- Full immutable audit log of every action and change (use the existing audit mechanism only if it can be used without modifying existing code; otherwise keep the module's own).
- Configurable settings: weight deviation threshold, quantity tolerance, max trucks inside plant, max trucks per bay, permit validity period.
- Blacklist management for drivers, vehicles and carriers.
